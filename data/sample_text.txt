KarunaAI
is a multi-modal, AI-driven platform designed to drastically reduce response times for all forms of
critical local issues, spanning distressed animals, infrastructure failures, public health hazards, 
and social welfare emergencies, ensuring rapid and targeted intervention across the civic spectrum by
automating the reporting, verification, and dispatch process. It transforms passive citizen reports 
into actionable, real-time service requests for Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and rescue centers.
